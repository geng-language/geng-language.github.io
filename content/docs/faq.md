## About the project

- Why create Geng instead of contributing to Gren?
- Why not Haskell, OCaml, F#, Gleam or Roc?
- What does "Geng" mean?
- Who is behind it, and is it ready for production? (Link to [Status](@/docs/status.md).)

## Language design

- Why no typeclasses / interfaces?
- Why no mutation, even locally?
- Why no exceptions?
- Why no tuples?
- Why `Array` instead of linked lists?
- Why can't I define my own operators?
- Why so few features overall?

## Platforms

- Why support five targets?
- Is the same program really identical on every target?
- Which target should I use?

## Practical

- How do I debug without print statements?
- How fast is Geng?
- Can I use Geng with my existing codebase? (Link to [Interop](@/docs/interop.md).)
- Is there editor support?
- Where is the standard library documentation? (Not yet: packages still being chosen.)
